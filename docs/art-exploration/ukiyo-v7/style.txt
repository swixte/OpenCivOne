Use only the ukiyo-e woodblock TECHNIQUE of the reference images, drawn as a true woodblock print: a bold black key-block outline of even weight around every shape, figure, icon and panel, as in a Hokusai or Hiroshige print; flat areas of colour inside the lines with no soft shading, no airbrushing and no painterly texture; soft bokashi gradients only in skies, water and title bands; cream washi paper with faint grain. Never a soft storybook or children's-book illustration.
Interface palette: sumi ink #1E1A16 for key lines and text, deep indigo #1B2740 and pale indigo #4E688C for panels and buttons, madder #8E3324 and vermilion for frames, seals and highlights, cream #F4ECD8 for paper and light text.
The people, buildings and objects belong to the civilization named, not to Japan, but panel frames and ornaments are neutral and the same for every civilization: thin madder rules, small cloud and wave motifs, no national patterns.
Banners and emblems are invented: no real national flags, no stars and stripes, no coats of arms, no inscriptions such as SPQR. Seals and stamps carry no writing of any script: a plain geometric mark (a square spiral, a lozenge, concentric squares).
Flat and printed, never photographic or 3D. Lettering is a clean, readable serif in sumi ink or cream, short, and spelled exactly as given in quotes.
