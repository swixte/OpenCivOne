A concept sheet for a Civilization-like strategy game, in exactly the same style as the reference images: four panels in a 2 by 2 grid on cream paper with thin cream gutters, each with a small plain black caption on the gutter below it. Each panel is a full game screen.
Panel 1, caption "Title": the title band "OpenCivOne" in large cream serif letters on deep indigo, with great Hokusai waves and clouds at both ends, across the middle of a calm dark indigo screen. Below it is the menu, written directly on a cream paper card with a madder frame, not in a grey box: "Start a New Game", "Load a Saved Game", "EARTH", "Customize World", "View Hall of Fame". The first line is highlighted with a pale indigo band and a small vermilion seal mark.
Panel 2, caption "Difficulty": a woodblock landscape at dawn on the left. On the right, on calm paper, is the heading "Difficulty Level" with five choices, one per line, each beside a small crown that grows from a simple circlet to an imperial crown: "Chieftain", "Warlord", "Prince", "King", "Emperor". "Prince" is highlighted.
Panel 3, caption "Choose your tribe": a row of seven tall hanging banners like nobori, each a different colour and emblem above a small figure of its people, labelled "Romans", "Babylonians", "Germans", "Egyptians", "Americans", "Greeks", "Indians". The Greek banner is raised and highlighted. Below the banners, the line "Your Leader: Alexander" is written in an entry field on paper with a text cursor.
Panel 4, caption "Customize world": a bird's-eye woodblock view of a young world of sea and continents. On the right, on calm paper, are four small choice groups, each with a heading and options and one option highlighted per group: "LAND MASS: Small, Normal, Large, Huge"; "TEMPERATURE: Cool, Temperate, Warm"; "CLIMATE: Arid, Normal, Wet"; "AGE: 3 billion years, 4 billion years, 5 billion years".
