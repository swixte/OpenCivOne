A concept sheet for a Civilization-like strategy game in exactly the same ukiyo-e woodblock style as the reference game screens: flat colour inside fine black key-block lines, indigo and Prussian blue, sage and ochre ground, vermilion accents, cream paper, Hokusai waves. The camera looks straight down, as on the reference map. Thin cream gutters separate the panels, and each panel has a small plain black caption on the cream gutter below it.
This sheet is one single large continuous map with no panels, showing every TERRAIN TYPE flowing into its neighbours with soft, natural borders and no visible grid: open sea with Hokusai waves, shallow coastal water, a beach, grassland, plains, desert dunes, tundra, arctic ice and snowfields, swamp with reeds, jungle, dense forest, rolling hills, snowy mountains, and a river winding from the mountains to the sea. Each terrain type has a small black caption written on a cream label placed on it. No units and no cities.
