A concept sheet for a Civilization-like strategy game, in exactly the same style as the reference images: three panels side by side on cream paper with thin cream gutters, each with a small plain black caption on the gutter below it.
The civilization is the Greeks, classical era. Greek subjects: chitons, columns, scrolls, an owl.
Panel 1, caption "Choose research": a game dialog drawn as an unrolled paper scroll with wooden rollers at the top and bottom, hung over a dimmed map. At its top left are the head and shoulders of a Greek science advisor, an old philosopher holding a scroll. The scroll reads "Which research field should our wise men be pursuing, sire?" and then lists four choices, one per line: "Astronomy", "Philosophy", "Monarchy", "The Wheel". "Astronomy" is highlighted with a pale indigo band and a small vermilion seal mark at its left.
Panel 2, caption "Science report": a full screen titled "SCIENCE REPORT" with "Empire of the Greeks" below it and a portrait of the same advisor at the top right. Under the title is a wide progress bar of small stamped oil lamps, 9 lit and 7 unlit, with the line "Researching Astronomy (72/128)". Below it, the advances already known stand in three tidy columns, each name with a tiny round woodblock emblem: Alphabet, Bronze Working, Ceremonial Burial, Code of Laws, Currency, Horseback Riding, Masonry, Pottery, Writing, Mysticism, Literacy, Mathematics.
Panel 3, caption "Advance discovered": a full-screen announcement. A woodblock picture of Greek astronomers on a rooftop with an armillary sphere, under a night sky of stylised stars, sits in a madder frame like a mounted print. Above it is the line "Greek wise men discover ASTRONOMY!" and below it the small line "Allows: Copernicus' Observatory, Navigation, University". A vermilion seal sits at the bottom right corner.
