A concept sheet for a Civilization-like strategy game in exactly the same ukiyo-e woodblock style as the reference images: flat colour inside fine black key-block lines, indigo and Prussian blue, sage and ochre, vermilion accents, cream paper with faint woodgrain, Hokusai waves and clouds. Thin cream gutters separate the panels, and each panel has a small plain black caption on the gutter below it.
PALACE AND CITY VIEW: six panels, 2 rows of 3.
Top row, the player's palace seen from the front across a garden, at three stages: a modest timber hall, a fine walled palace with towers, and a magnificent palace with gardens, gates and pagodas or domes.
Bottom row, the city view, a wide three-quarter view of a city's buildings on a hillside: an ancient city with temple, granary and walls; a medieval city with cathedral, market and castle; an industrial city with factories, railway and bank.
