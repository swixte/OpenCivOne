A sheet of SIXTEEN separate small Babylonian courtyard houses, four rows of four, each with wide magenta gaps, drawn exactly like the houses in the reference painting: low mud-brick compounds seen from above with open courtyards, flat roofs, a doorway, some with a palm or a small garden in the court, a few two-storey; all the same small scale, each footprint a small diamond about one-fifth of the palace's width.
