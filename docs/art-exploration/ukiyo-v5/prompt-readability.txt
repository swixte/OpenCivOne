A readability test sheet for a Civilization-like strategy game: a grid of 12 square map panels, 4 columns by 3 rows, thin cream gutters, a small black caption under each. Use only the ukiyo-e woodblock TECHNIQUE of the references: fine black key-block outlines, flat colour areas, soft bokashi gradients, patterned foliage and waves, cream paper texture. The SUBJECTS are not Japanese: people, buildings and ships belong to the civilization shown (Greek, Egyptian, Roman, Persian, medieval European and so on). Camera straight down on the map, like the reference game screen. No grid lines.
Each panel is one terrain type filling the whole panel, and on it the same three things: a small city on its cleared-earth ring, one unit token, and one land improvement where it makes sense. Panels: Grassland (irrigated fields), Plains (irrigated fields), Desert (irrigation from an oasis), Forest (a road), Jungle (a road), Hills (a mine), Mountains (a mine), Tundra (a road), Arctic (a road), Swamp (a road), River valley (irrigated fields), Coast (a trireme instead of a land unit).
Each terrain has its own clearly different colour AND brightness, so they never blend together: deep sea dark indigo #1F3A5F with Hokusai waves; shallow coast bright turquoise #4F9BA8; grassland fresh light green #8DB255; plains warm golden ochre #D9B45A; desert pale sand #EAD9A6 with dune lines; forest very dark blue-green #2E5A43 dense canopy; jungle saturated emerald #2F8A4E with palms; hills olive-brown #A08A4E with contour lines; mountains slate grey-blue #6E7C8C with white snow caps; tundra grey-lilac #A9A39A; arctic white #F2F2EE with pale blue shadows; swamp dark teal-olive #4F6B55 with reed hatching and dark pools; rivers bright blue #3F7FBF with a thin light edge. Terrain colours are muted to medium saturation; vermilion, gold and strong player colours are NOT used on terrain.
Cities blend in but still pop: each city sits in a ring of light cleared earth #E8D9B0 that fades out into the land, with lanes and outlying houses; its roofs are vermilion and terracotta and its walls cream with a crisp dark key line (vermilion is used ONLY for cities, so they are the brightest warm spot on the map); a soft shadow on the lower right; a small player-coloured size badge. A city fills about one map square, larger cities a little more.
Land improvements are painted into the terrain in a tone of the ground they are on: irrigation is a slightly brighter green patchwork of fields with thin bright-blue water channels following the land; mines are a dark spoil terrace with a small timber headframe; roads are thin pale cream dirt lines; railroads are thin dark lines with sleepers.
Units are small flat print tokens, about half a map square, with a thin cream outline and a strong saturated player colour (blue, red, yellow, white, purple, green) on their flag and clothing, so they stand out on every terrain. Use a different player colour per row: blue, red, yellow.
