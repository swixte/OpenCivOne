A user-interface kit sheet for a Civilization-like strategy game, in exactly the same style as the reference images: every interface component laid out neatly on one large cream paper sheet with thin cream gutters, each with a small plain black caption below it. Components are shown flat and straight on, at game size, ready to be cut out.
Row 1, "Frames": an empty paper panel with a thin madder frame and a corner ornament; the same panel with an indigo title band reading "CITY RESOURCES"; an empty deep indigo panel with a thin cream key line; a title cartouche reading "MILITARY ADVISOR"; a horizontal divider made of a narrow band of stylised waves.
Row 2, "Dialogs": a hanging-scroll message box with wooden rollers and a red cord at the top, reading "Pollution near Thebes." with a vermilion seal button "OK"; a choice dialog as an unrolled scroll reading "Do you want to sell your Barracks?" with two lines, "Yes" and "No", and "Yes" highlighted with a pale indigo band.
Row 3, "Menus": a menu bar on cream paper with the words "GAME", "ORDERS", "ADVISORS", "WORLD", "CIVILOPEDIA", with "GAME" pressed in madder; under it, its drop-down as a short paper scroll with the lines "Tax Rate", "Luxuries", "Find City", "Options", "Save Game", "Quit", and one line highlighted.
Row 4, "Buttons and tabs": a rectangular indigo seal button "CHANGE" in three states (normal, hover lighter, pressed darker and inset); a vermilion seal button "BUY" in the same three states; a square vermilion seal "EXIT"; the buttons keep the clear order of the first reference image, one row of three for CHANGE and one row of three for BUY; a row of four tabs, "INFO", "HAPPY", "MAP", "VIEW", with "HAPPY" active; a small round count seal with the number "3"; a city size badge with "8".
Row 5, "Bars and icons": a progress bar of stamped shields, 7 of 10 filled; a progress bar of grain bundles; a progress bar of oil lamps; and each resource icon alone at small size: grain bundle, round shield, square-holed coin, gold coin, oil lamp, luxury vase, and three tiny woodblock citizen heads in profile with patterned headcloths, one frowning, one calm and one smiling (not round emoji faces, not yellow smileys).
Row 6, "Ledger": a small report table on paper with a madder header rule, three columns "Unit", "Strength", "Count", and three rows "Phalanx 1/2/1 4", "Legion 4/2/1 2", "Trireme 1/1/3 1", with alternate rows faintly tinted.
