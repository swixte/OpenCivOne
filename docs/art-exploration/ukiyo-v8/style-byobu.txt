STYLE: a Japanese rakuchu-rakugai folding screen of the Momoyama period, the tradition of painting whole cities in parallel projection. Bands of raised gold-leaf clouds (suyari-gasumi) drift across the city and part to reveal it, framing the buildings and hiding the edges of the scene. Thick opaque mineral pigments: malachite green for trees and fields, azurite blue for water and the glazed gates, shell-white gofun for walls, earth ochre for mud-brick, touches of cinnabar. Fine but confident ink outlines, flat colour fields, gold ground showing through. Faint folds of the screen panels visible as soft vertical lines. Luxurious, calm, ceremonial.
