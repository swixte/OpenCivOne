A unit readability test sheet for a Civilization-like strategy game, in exactly the woodblock style of the reference images (fine black key-block lines, flat colour, cream paper). A grid of 8 columns by 5 rows of small square map panels with thin cream gutters.
Rows are terrains, each panel filled edge to edge with that terrain, top to bottom: Arctic (white ice #F2F2EE), Desert (pale sand #EAD9A6), Plains (golden ochre #D9B45A), Grassland (fresh green #8DB255), Tundra (lilac-grey #B4A3B8). A small black caption at the left of each row.
Columns are the 8 players, each with the same unit, a spearman, standing in the middle of the panel and about half its size, holding a flag in the player colour, with clothing trimmed in that colour. Captions above each column: Red, White, Green, Blue, Yellow, Cyan, Pink, Grey. Player colours are strong and saturated, much stronger than the muted terrain: red #E8483F, white #FFFFFF with a dark border on the flag, green #2FC04A, blue #3E5BFF, yellow #FFD21A, cyan #2FC8E0, pink #E84AC8, grey a dark ink grey #4A4A50.
EVERY unit has a double outline around its whole silhouette: a dark key line #1E1A16 directly around the figure and a thin cream outline #F4ECD8 outside it, so it reads on both light and dark ground.
