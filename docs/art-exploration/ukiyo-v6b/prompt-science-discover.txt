One full game screen for a Civilization-like strategy game, landscape, filling the image edge to edge, in the style of the reference images: the ADVANCE DISCOVERED announcement.
The civilization is the Greeks, classical era.
On the left, about three fifths of the screen: a woodblock print of Greek astronomers on a rooftop at night, an old astronomer pointing at the stars, a younger one turning an armillary sphere and a scribe writing, under a deep indigo sky of stylised stars with a bokashi gradient, temples and cypresses behind. The print sits in a madder frame like a mounted print.
On the right, on calm cream paper: the line "Greek wise men discover" above the large word "ASTRONOMY!", then a thin rule, then the small heading "Allows" with three lines: "Copernicus' Observatory", "Navigation", "University". At the bottom right a vermilion seal with a plain geometric mark, no writing.
