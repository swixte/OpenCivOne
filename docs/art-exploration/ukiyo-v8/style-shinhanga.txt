STYLE: a shin-hanga woodblock print in the manner of Yoshida Hiroshi and Kawase Hasui: painterly and atmospheric, built from many carefully overlapped transparent colour blocks so the colour looks brushed and glowing, still with clean shapes and soft key lines. Golden late-afternoon light from the left, long soft shadows, warm light on the mud-brick, cool blue-violet in the shadows, a hazy luminous sky, reflections in the river. Subtle gradations instead of hatching, a few areas of fine detail at the focal point only. Calm, warm, beautiful, like a print you would frame.
