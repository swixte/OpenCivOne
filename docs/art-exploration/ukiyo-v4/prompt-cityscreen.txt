A concept sheet for a Civilization-like strategy game in exactly the same ukiyo-e woodblock style as the reference images: flat colour inside fine black key-block lines, indigo and Prussian blue, sage and ochre, vermilion accents, cream paper with faint woodgrain, Hokusai waves and clouds. Thin cream gutters separate the panels, and each panel has a small plain black caption on the gutter below it.
CITY SCREEN: one full game screen for managing a city, in the layout of the classic 1991 Civilization city screen, all drawn as a woodblock print on cream paper with vermilion frames:
top left, a strip of citizens as tiny woodblock figures (content, happy, unhappy); below it rows of resource icons for food (rice bundles), shields (small round shields) and trade (coins); in the centre a small top-down map of the city's surroundings with its worked squares; on the right a list of buildings with small icons; bottom right a production box showing a Phalanx being built with a progress bar of shields; a granary box with stored food; at the top the city name "ROME" with size 8 and the year.
