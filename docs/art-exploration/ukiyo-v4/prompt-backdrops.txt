A concept sheet for a Civilization-like strategy game in exactly the same ukiyo-e woodblock style as the reference images: flat colour inside fine black key-block lines, indigo and Prussian blue, sage and ochre, vermilion accents, cream paper with faint woodgrain, Hokusai waves and clouds. Thin cream gutters separate the panels, and each panel has a small plain black caption on the gutter below it.
FULL-SCREEN PICTURES: six wide panels, 2 rows of 3, each a woodblock landscape print with a calm empty area for text:
The birth of the world (a molten young Earth under a starry sky), The first village (huts by a river at dawn), Choose difficulty (five crowns from an iron circlet to an imperial crown on a velvet cloth), Throne room (a ruler's hall where an envoy is received), A great discovery (a scholar's study at night with instruments), The fall (ruins of a great city overgrown by forest).
