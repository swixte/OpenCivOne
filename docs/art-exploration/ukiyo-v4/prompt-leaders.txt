A concept sheet for a Civilization-like strategy game in exactly the same ukiyo-e woodblock style as the reference images: flat colour inside fine black key-block lines, indigo and Prussian blue, sage and ochre, vermilion accents, cream paper with faint woodgrain, Hokusai waves and clouds. Thin cream gutters separate the panels, and each panel has a small plain black caption on the gutter below it.
LEADERS: fourteen head-and-shoulders portraits in two rows of seven, each in a vermilion frame on cream, in the manner of a woodblock actor portrait, each an individual face with period clothing and a calm or slightly stern expression:
Elizabeth I, Ramesses II, Mahatma Gandhi, Shaka Zulu, Abraham Lincoln, Genghis Khan, Mao Zedong; Hammurabi, Joseph Stalin, Montezuma, Julius Caesar, Napoleon Bonaparte, Frederick the Great, Alexander the Great.
