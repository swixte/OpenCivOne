A sprite sheet of ancient Babylonian CITY WALL pieces for a strategy game, isolated with wide magenta gaps:
row 1: a STRAIGHT WALL SEGMENT running diagonally from upper-left to lower-right along the isometric axis (mud-brick, crenellated top, about four times as long as it is tall); the SAME SEGMENT running diagonally from upper-right to lower-left;
row 2: a square CORNER TOWER slightly taller than the wall; a CITY GATE in a straight wall running upper-right to lower-left, with two tall towers of blue-glazed brick decorated with yellow lions and an arched opening.
