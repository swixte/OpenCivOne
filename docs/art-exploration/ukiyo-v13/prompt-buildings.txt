A sheet of SEVEN separate buildings of ancient Babylon for a strategy game's town screen, each drawn in a FRONTAL view from a low viewpoint at about eye level, slightly from the left (like a stage flat: we see the front face and a little of one side and the roof), standing on a thin strip of its own ochre ground. Two rows, wide magenta gaps between them:
row 1: the TEMPLE, a tall seven-stepped ziggurat with blue-glazed upper tiers, a long central stairway and a small blue shrine on top (the tallest building, twice as tall as it is wide); the PALACE, a broad two-storey mud-brick palace with a blue-glazed gate and lion reliefs, crenellated parapets and palms behind; the LIBRARY, a hall with a portico of columns and a blue frieze;
row 2: the GRANARY, a long storehouse with a row of tall domed grain bins; the BARRACKS, a walled yard with a gate, a watchtower and spears visible over the wall; the MARKETPLACE under construction, timber scaffolding with workers over half-built stalls; the CITY GATE, the blue Ishtar gate with yellow aurochs and dragon reliefs flanked by two short stretches of crenellated wall.
Isolated on a perfectly flat, uniform magenta (#FF00FF) background; no shadows on the magenta.
