One full game screen for a Civilization-like strategy game: the CITY SCREEN, seen straight on, filling the image edge to edge, in exactly the same style as the reference images.
The city is BABYLON of the Babylonians, ancient era, size 8, in 1250 BC. Babylonian subjects: glazed-brick walls, a ziggurat, robes and beards.
Layout: every panel is cream paper with a thin madder frame and an indigo title band, and narrow gutters separate the panels.
TOP BAND across the full width: the title "BABYLON (Population: 210,000)". Below it is a single row of 8 small citizen figures in this order: 3 happy (raised arms), 3 content, 1 unhappy (head down), and, set slightly apart, 1 specialist, an entertainer with a lute.
LEFT COLUMN, top: a panel titled "City Resources" with three rows of small stamped icons. Food is bundles of grain (12 icons, the last 4 drawn hollow to show the surplus). Shields are small round shields (6 icons). Trade is square-holed coins (8 icons). Under the trade row is a split row: 2 luxury vases, 3 gold coins and 3 science oil lamps. Below the panel is a strip titled "Units Supported" with 3 tiny unit tokens: a spearman, a chariot and a settler.
CENTRE: the city map, a square top-down patch of painted ukiyo-e terrain (river, grassland, plains, hills, a strip of coast). It shows the 21 squares around the city in a rounded diamond, with the city in the middle under vermilion roofs. 8 worked squares carry tiny stamped yield icons (grain, shield, coin); the rest are plain. There are no grid lines, only a faint outline around the worked area.
CENTRE, below the map: an info panel with four tabs on top, "INFO", "HAPPY", "MAP" and "VIEW". INFO is highlighted as a pressed indigo tab. Inside, the units in the city stand as small tokens in a row (a spearman fortified behind a palisade, an archer), each with a tiny caption.
RIGHT COLUMN: a list of city improvements, each with a small woodblock building icon and its name: Palace, Granary, Temple, Barracks, City Walls, Library. At the bottom are two small buttons, "MORE" and "WONDERS".
BOTTOM LEFT: a "Food Storage" box drawn as a woven granary basket holding rows of grain bundles, about two thirds full.
BOTTOM RIGHT: the production box. It holds a large woodblock picture of a chariot being built, the label "Chariot", and a progress bar of stamped shields, 14 filled and 6 empty. Below them, three buttons in a row, "CHANGE", "BUY" and "AUTO", are drawn as rectangular seal stamps (CHANGE and AUTO indigo, BUY vermilion).
BOTTOM EDGE: two buttons, "RENAME" and "EXIT", with EXIT as a vermilion seal at the far right.
Everything reads at a glance, generous but compact. No decorative border runs around the whole screen.
