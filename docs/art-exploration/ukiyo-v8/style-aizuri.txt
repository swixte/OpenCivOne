STYLE: an aizuri-e, a woodblock print printed almost entirely in Prussian blue. Four tones of blue from pale wash to deep indigo, sumi black for key lines, the cream paper, and a single small accent of vermilion on the palace roof and the gate banners. Strong graphic shapes, bold flat areas of blue, crisp carved outlines, stylised Hokusai water patterns on the river and sea, a gradated blue sky. Visible paper fibre and woodgrain. Striking, restrained, elegant.
