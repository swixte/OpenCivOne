STYLE: a Hiroshige landscape woodblock print from the Hundred Famous Views series, printed by hand. Five or six inks only: Prussian blue, a soft green, pale ochre, sumi black, a little vermilion, and the untouched cream paper. A deep bokashi gradient of Prussian blue at the top of the sky fading to bare paper, and another along the river and sea. Buildings simplified into clean flat planes with carved key lines that vary in weight, thicker in the foreground. Large areas of plain paper. Visible woodgrain and the soft mottling of a baren-rubbed print, slight misregistration of colour blocks. Quiet, poetic, airy.
