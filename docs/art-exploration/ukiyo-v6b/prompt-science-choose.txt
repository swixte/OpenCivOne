One full game screen for a Civilization-like strategy game, landscape, filling the image edge to edge, in the style of the reference images: the CHOOSE RESEARCH dialog over the game.
The civilization is the Greeks, classical era. Greek subjects: chitons, columns, scrolls, an owl.
Behind the dialog is the main game screen, dimmed: the painted ukiyo-e map with Greek towns and a trireme on the sea, and the cream sidebar on the left. In the centre hangs an unrolled paper scroll with wooden rollers at the top and bottom and a red cord, about a third of the screen wide, like the reference dialog.
At the top left of the scroll are the head and shoulders of a Greek science advisor, an old philosopher holding a scroll. Beside him the scroll reads "Which research field should our wise men be pursuing, sire?". Below, four choices, one per line, separated by thin rules: "Astronomy", "Philosophy", "Monarchy", "The Wheel". "Astronomy" is highlighted with a pale indigo band and a small vermilion seal with a plain geometric mark at its left.
