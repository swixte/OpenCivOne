A concept sheet for a Civilization-like strategy game in exactly the same ukiyo-e woodblock style as the reference images: flat colour inside fine black key-block lines, indigo and Prussian blue, sage and ochre, vermilion accents, cream paper with faint woodgrain, Hokusai waves and clouds. Thin cream gutters separate the panels, and each panel has a small plain black caption on the gutter below it.
ENCYCLOPEDIA PICTURES: six wide illustration panels, 2 rows of 3, each a single clear scene with one to three figures, an eye-level view and a simple setting, all in the same woodblock print style for every era:
Pottery (a potter at a wheel), Bronze Working (smiths pouring bronze), Philosophy (two philosophers debating under a pine), Gunpowder (musketeers firing a volley), Railroad (a steam train crossing a bridge over a river), Space Flight (a rocket lifting off, drawn as a woodblock print).
