The OpenCivOne city screen, laid out as Civ1's. Every panel sits at its original 320×200 position times four, read from CityWorker.cs, and its behaviour follows that file: unit upkeep, corruption, trade routes, the HAPPY rows, selling, buying, AUTO and the keys. Only the city view (VIEW tab) and the city map are art, in gold frames. The city view is a town screen seen from the front: a painted backdrop with each improvement as a cut-out at its own place and depth, and the city filling in as it grows: houses packed inside the walls, then suburbs on the plain, with more people, carts, stalls and boats.
Keys: C change · B buy · Shift+A auto · I / H / M / V tabs · A activate a unit · S sell · P map cursor (arrows move it, Space works the square) · R rename · Esc exit.
