A concept sheet for a Civilization-like strategy game in exactly the same ukiyo-e woodblock style as the reference game screens: flat colour inside fine black key-block lines, indigo and Prussian blue, sage and ochre ground, vermilion accents, cream paper, Hokusai waves. The camera looks straight down, as on the reference map. Thin cream gutters separate the panels, and each panel has a small plain black caption on the cream gutter below it.
The sheet shows TERRAIN IMPROVEMENTS as a grid of 12 map panels, 3 rows by 4 columns, all painted into the ground as part of the print, never stamped on as tiles, never a checkerboard:
1 Irrigation by a river: curving hedged fields and paddies following the river. 2 Irrigation on open plains: a patchwork of irregular fields with dikes and a few farmhouses. 3 Irrigation reaching a city: fields fanning out from a walled town. 4 Mine in hills: a dug terrace with spoil heaps and a timber headframe. 5 Mine in mountains: tunnel mouths and tracks on a slope. 6 Road: a thin dirt road winding across grass and over a small bridge. 7 Railroad: a thin dark rail line with sleepers, crossing a river on a bridge. 8 Road and railroad meeting at a small station. 9 Fortress: low earthwork ramparts with a stockade, sitting in the ground. 10 Pollution: grey-brown smog stains spreading over fields. 11 Village hut: a few thatched huts in a clearing. 12 Plain grassland with nothing on it, for comparison.
